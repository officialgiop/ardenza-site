Support

Have a question, a problem or a suggestion about Ardenza? Here are the most common answers and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email: alfopietro@gmail.com

To help you faster, please tell us:

  • your iPhone model (and Apple Watch, if you use one) and the iOS version;
  • the Ardenza version: you find it in Settings, in the app card, under "Version";
  • what you were doing and what happened.

Please do not send us your health data or screenshots with personal information you do not want to share. We have no access to the app's data: it stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

The language is chosen once, at first launch, and cannot be changed afterwards. To pick another one you have to delete the app and reinstall it: you will lose saved workouts and cards, unless you restore them from a device backup.

The Watch app does not appear on my watch

  • Check that the iPhone and Apple Watch are paired, unlocked and with Bluetooth on.
  • On the iPhone open the Watch app, "My Watch" section, find Ardenza among the apps and tap Install.
  • Restart the iPhone and the Watch and try again.
  • Ardenza also works without an Apple Watch: the Watch is optional.

I do not see heart rate during a workout

Heart rate comes from Apple Watch and needs Health permission.

  • On iPhone: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Ardenza, and allow reading heart rate and active energy.
  • Start the workout choosing "Apple Watch" before you begin.
  • Without an Apple Watch, the app does not show heart rate.

How do I save workouts to Health?

In Settings turn on "Export to Health". Workouts managed by Apple Watch are saved to Health like in any workout app. If you delete a workout from the app, the copy in Health stays, unless you choose "Also delete from Health".

The app closed during a workout

When you reopen it you will find "Resume interrupted workout": you can continue from where you were, save what you did, or discard the session.

I changed iPhone and my workouts are gone

The data stays on the device and there is no account to sync it. To bring it to the new iPhone, restore the new phone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or a computer), or use Apple's direct transfer.

How do I delete my data?

  • One workout: open it from Results and delete it.
  • Custom cards and exercises: delete them from the Custom section.
  • Everything: delete the app from the device.

Details are in the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

I found a mistake in an exercise or a translation

Write to us at the address above, telling us the exercise or card and what is not correct. It helps us improve the app.

I am trying the app with TestFlight

To report a problem you can use TestFlight: tap Send Feedback from the TestFlight app, even with a screenshot. If you have a crash, Apple sends us the report if you turned on analytics sharing.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later (optional).
