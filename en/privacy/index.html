Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Ardenza ("the app") handles your data. In short: your data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We do not receive it and we have no access to it.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-up, no login.
  • Workouts, cards, profile and notes stay on your device.
  • No advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have it.
  • Health data (heart rate and active energy) is read and saved only if you choose, and it stays in Health on your device.
  • The app works without a connection.

Because the data never leaves your device, the controller does not receive, store or read what you enter or record in the app.

What data the app uses and where it stays

All the data below is saved only on your device, in the space reserved for the app.

  • Profile (optional): name, sex, height, weight and fitness level. They personalize the texts and suggest a starting level. You can skip the questions and review them any time.
  • Preferences: language, theme, sound cues and haptics, preferred division and device.
  • Workouts: date and duration, segment and rest times, outcome (completed or stopped), title, notes, favorites and the link to a race.
  • Heart rate and active energy: only if recorded with Apple Watch or read from Health, and only with your permission.
  • Custom cards and exercises: the circuits and exercises you create.
  • Your races: the name, date and place you enter yourself.

The language chosen at first launch is saved on the device and cannot be changed without reinstalling the app.

Apple Watch

If you use Apple Watch, the iPhone and the Watch exchange workout information directly with each other, through Apple's connection. The data does not go through our servers.

Health (HealthKit)

The app asks for access to Health only when needed:

  • Read: heart rate and active energy during a workout with Apple Watch, to show them live and in the summary.
  • Write: the completed workout. With the iPhone only, just if you turn on "Export to Health". With Apple Watch, the workout is saved to Health like in any workout app.

Health data is not used for advertising or profiling and is not shared with third parties. You can change or revoke permissions in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

If you delete a workout from the app, the copy already saved in Health stays, unless you choose "Also delete from Health". We do not delete Health data created by other apps.

Backups

The app's data may be included in the backups of your device that you make with Apple (iCloud or a computer), according to your settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you: we do not access them. The app does not use iCloud or any other sync service of its own.

Network connections

The app works offline. It connects to the internet in these cases:

  • When you open a link. The Shop shows suggestions for equipment and accessories with links to Amazon.it; other sections may open official pages (for example a race calendar) or the App Store. Links open in your browser. From then on, the destination site handles your data under its own policy (for example, Amazon may use cookies). The app does not send these sites your workouts, heart rate or profile data. Amazon links may include an affiliate code that tells Amazon you came from Ardenza: if you buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. The code contains no data about you.
  • Content updates (if active in your version). The app may download two small text files from a public address: the Shop catalog and a message to show on Home (for example an update notice). It is a plain read request: we send none of your data. The service hosting the files (GitHub Pages) can see your IP address and the standard technical data of any web request, as with any website.

The app uses no advertising, no tracking tools, no social features, and no third-party libraries for usage or crash analytics.

Permissions

The app does not use location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos or Bluetooth. It may ask for access to Health (see above). During a workout you can keep the screen on: it is an app setting, not a permission.

TestFlight

If you try a test version with TestFlight, Apple may collect and give us information such as crash reports, feedback and install statistics, according to your device's TestFlight settings. They do not contain your workout data.

How to delete your data

  • One workout: open it from Results and delete it.
  • Custom cards and exercises: delete them from the Custom section.
  • Everything: uninstall the app from your device: the app's data is deleted. If you have a backup, the copy stays in the backup for as long as you keep it.
  • Health: data saved in Health is managed from the Health app.

Your rights

Since we neither receive nor keep your data, access, correction, deletion and portability are done directly in the app and on your device. For questions or requests write to alfopietro@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14 and does not collect personal data: everything stays on the device. If a minor uses it, we recommend doing so with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we update this page and the date above. For important changes we also say so in the app.
